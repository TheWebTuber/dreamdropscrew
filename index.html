Salahare (Sal)

When my ship, Kirni, crashed into JD Forest, the stars seems to whisper. I came for the spirits’ radiant energy, but my landing shattered their home. Now I wander the wreckage, collecting fragments while the forest hums with their sorrow. Every pulse I take feels like a confession—an accident, or a crime I can’t undo. Now i wondering in this place with my space robot buddy.

Blackjacksetzer (Blackjack)

Ever since Sal’s ship came crashing down in JD Forest, nothing’s been the same. The blast flattened a small wooden home hidden among the trees — a home that belonged to an ancient fox spirit. Now, stripped of his sanctuary, he prowls the forest with burning eyes and a twisted grin. Magic crackles in his wake, turning peace to panic, and laughter to screams. He calls it justice. The rest of us call it revenge. But deep down, I can’t tell if he’s punishing Sal… or all of us who let his forest burn.

Crosshair

He stands behind the bar with steady hands and a tired smile, pouring warmth for strangers while his eyes count exits. By night he listens, collects secrets in empty glasses — and when no one watches, another voice answers: IN THE NAME OF THE SUN, I WOULD NEVER BOW TO NO ONE, BUT I WILL HEED YOUR ADVICE AND FACE MY TRUE ENEMIES AS A BEAST. The barkeep is a mask; beneath it prowls a hungry other, a guardian and a predator at once, ready to trade a story for a favor — or to unleash the thing that never learned to apologize.

Portal Master Of Games (The Wolf)

From the edge of every tale, the Wolf watches—hidden in the folds of shadow, where stories dare not look back. He gazes upon others for his own amusement, weaving chaos and ruin into their paths just to see how they’ll fall. When a story grows dull, he twists its fate, burns its ending, and writes it anew with a smile no one truly understands. Some say he does it for fun… others whisper he has no choice. For even in his laughter, there’s a silence that does not belong to him.
